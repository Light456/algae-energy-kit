Product description

Real algae. Real electricity. Real science you can see.

Unlock the science of renewable energy with the Algae Energy Kit, a hands‑on STEM experiment that turns living algae into a working micro‑power generator. Designed for students, educators, and curious makers, this kit transforms real algae‑bioelectricity research into an accessible, safe, and engaging learning experience.

Inside the photobioreactor chamber, algae grow and begin producing tiny amounts of electricity through natural biological processes. The included electrode module captures this current, and the LED display board shows your power output in real time. Users can adjust light levels, nutrients, and airflow to see how biological conditions affect energy production.

The kit also introduces the concept of carbon capture and biological carbon credits, showing how algae absorb CO₂ and how living systems can contribute to measurable climate solutions. A Carbon Credit Tracker Card and QR‑linked learning site provide easy, age‑appropriate explanations.

Whether used in classrooms, science fairs, sustainability clubs, or home maker projects, the Algae Energy Kit delivers a unique blend of biology, engineering, and environmental science. With modular components, clear instructions, and safety‑first design, it's an ideal entry point into renewable energy education.

Enhanced Brand Content

See exactly how living algae become a working power source.

Grow Power from Living Algae

Algae do more than grow. As they photosynthesize, they release tiny electrical charges. The carbon cloth anode collects them, the current travels through the circuit, and the LED lights up. It’s renewable energy you can watch happen.

What You'll Build

Eight reusable components snap together into a working bio‑power station. Color‑coded red and black leads, a clear acrylic chamber and an illustrated booklet make the first build simple, even for first‑time makers.

See Your Energy Output

Your algae. Your electricity. The LED display board lights up as current flows, so you can see the results in real time. Change the light, nutrients or airflow and watch the output respond, or connect a multimeter to log your own data.

Learn Carbon Capture & Credits

Every bubble carries CO₂ to the algae, which lock the carbon into new growth and release oxygen. Track your culture on the Carbon Credit Tracker Card and scan its QR code to learn how living systems support real climate solutions.

Perfect for Classrooms & Makers

Five clear steps take students from an empty chamber to glowing results. Shaped by feedback from 10 student and educator beta testers, the kit fits science classes, sustainability clubs, science fairs and home projects.
